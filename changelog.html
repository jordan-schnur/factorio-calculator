1.4.0

5 October 2026

New

  • The planet picker moves from Settings to the top bar: one icon per planet, with the chosen one's name beside it. Click picks a planet; shift-click adds another.
  • Stacked belts. Settings → Machines → Belt has a Stacked choice, up to 4 items per spot as stack inserters build them, and every belt count, graph line and "belts" target follows it.
  • The table can be packed to the left (Settings → Display → Table), so on a wide screen each item's rate and machines sit next to its name. This browser remembers the choice.
  • The footer shows what the plan would take without modules: "Without modules: 230 machines · 27.8 MW".

Fixed

  • Old links with more than 16 beacons read as 16, and a fraction of a beacon rounds to the nearest whole one instead of reading as 0.
  • Swapping a module into a filled beacon slot keeps a beacon count of 0 at 0.
  • A row's Every row choice no longer comes back after the page restores your last factory.
  • A graph card answering a hovered line keeps its machine's quality badge, and the quality pickers keep keyboard focus after a pick.

1.3.0

5 October 2026

New

  • Machine quality. Pick normal to legendary for each machine in Settings → Machines, or on an open row's Machine line, and the machine counts follow the faster crafting speed. Mining drills don't change, as in the game.
  • Module and beacon quality. The module editor and Settings → Modules take a quality for each module and for the beacons themselves, with the game's bonuses: productivity 3 gives 10, 13, 16, 19 or 25%.
  • With the companion app, From your save sets each machine to the quality most of its copies on that planet are built at.
  • Byproducts. When a plan makes something nothing uses, such as the heavy and light oil from advanced oil processing with cracking off, a bar above the plan says which machines will back up and stop, and offers fixes it has already worked out: allow cracking, use basic oil processing, or send the oil out. Each fix says how many machines it adds or saves, and Undo puts the plan back.
  • Advanced oil processing and other recipes with several outputs are named for themselves in the table and on the graph, with an icon for each output. On the graph, anything left over runs into a Left over card, or a Send out card once you send it out.

1.2.0

4 October 2026

New

  • Modules per row and per machine. Open a row to set its modules and beacons, for just that recipe or for every row made in the same machine, such as every foundry. A row set by hand wins over its machine, and a machine wins over the plan.
  • Every row shows its modules: a Modules column in the table, a strip on each graph card, and the lists in Settings.
  • Settings → Modules sets the plan as a strategy: productivity wherever it's allowed, speed or efficiency where it isn't, and beacons around each machine.
  • An open row compares its machines and power with no modules, with the plan's modules and with its own.

Fixed

  • Quality modules can no longer go in beacons, as in the game.
  • Beacon modules with a beacon count of 0 no longer break the plan.

1.1.0

2 October 2026

New

  • The search box knows the abbreviations players type: GC, RC, BC, PU, LDS, RF, UG, prod3 and more. "gc 60" asks for 60 green circuits a minute.
  • Plan by machine count: "yellow science 7 machines" plans for seven machines instead of a rate.
  • The landing screen shows screenshots of real plans behind the search box.

Fixed

  • Machine counts no longer go negative with lots of productivity modules. Speed now stops at −80%, as it does in the game; eight productivity modules in a cryogenic plant used to show a negative count.
  • Icons no longer show slices of their neighbours at some sizes.
  • The search results list draws over the landing screen text again instead of behind it.

1.0.0

2 October 2026

First public release at factoriocalculator.app.

  • Factorio 2.0 and Space Age: pick an item and a rate and see every machine, belt and raw input.
  • A table of everything the build needs, grouped into what you build here, what you bring in and what you mine or pipe in. Click a row for its ingredients, where it goes, its recipes and exact machine counts.
  • A flow graph of the same plan. Hover a line for its belts and how many machines on each side it feeds.
  • Plans only with machines you can build on the chosen planet, and lets you pin a recipe to a machine by hand.
  • Belt counts as fractions ("1/3 belt"), and a colour-blind mode that writes the colour on belt and inserter icons.
  • Kirk McDonald calculator links open here with the same plan.

Built on Kirk McDonald's calculator (Apache-2.0).
